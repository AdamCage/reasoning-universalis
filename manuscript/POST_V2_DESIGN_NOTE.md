# Completion Censoring and Completed-Error Support: A Post-v2 Offline Design

Offline development proposal following the closed [v2/R1 pilot](../reports/PILOT_V2_RECOVERY_RESULT.md). A [candidate protocol](../design/PILOT_V3_CANDIDATE_PROTOCOL_2026_11_08.json), deterministic [48-task preview](../artifacts/pilot-v3-design-2026-11-08/development-preview.json), [192-cell proposed roster](../artifacts/pilot-v3-design-2026-11-08/proposed-roster.json) and [manifest](../artifacts/pilot-v3-design-2026-11-08/manifest.json) are now prepared. There are no new model observations, human annotations or scientific approval.

## What the next design can distinguish

The preceding pilot encountered two observable barriers. Output-limit truncation prevented many final answers; among completed, gradeable answers, eligible errors remained scarce. At 8192 tokens, DeepSeek completed 37/48 attempts without a completed error, while GPT-OSS completed 35/48. Its four completed errors occupied three roots, but the fixed 256-word prefix retained only three arithmetic error traces from two roots. The planning error with only 40 pre-marker words was excluded. These observations motivate a new development question rather than a change to the old thresholds.

The new [selection analysis](../reports/POST_V2_SELECTION_ANALYSIS.md) distinguishes short raw reasoning from loss at the first answer marker. Of DeepSeek's 16 completed-but-ineligible responses at 8192, only two had fewer than 256 raw reasoning words; 14 had at least 256 raw words but fewer than 256 before `ANSWER:`. Both GPT-OSS exclusions had this latter pattern. In paired task comparisons, the higher cap gained completion on 23 DeepSeek and 24 GPT-OSS tasks, without observed completion losses, yet lost primary-prefix eligibility on six and one tasks respectively. These are descriptive transitions, not monotonic eligibility or causal effects. They do not authorize changing the censor, restoring its suffix or making an exception for a short error.

The next report should distinguish accepted completion, gradeability, prefix availability and final-answer correctness. Every model/cap denominator retains all 48 planned attempts, with explicit categories for correct completion, incorrect completion, unparseable completion, output-limit truncation, operational exclusion and unsent cells. Correctness is undefined for the primary analysis when completion or grading is unavailable. Diagnostic answers extracted from truncated outputs remain separate. This accounting can show which observed barrier limits support; it cannot recover the correctness of a hypothetical continuation or prove that errors would remain rare without a cap.

## Fixed prospective allocation

The allocation retains four domains, three construction tiers, two original roots per domain/tier and two dependent presentation variants: 24 root groups and 48 tasks. Crossing two intended model identities with output caps 8192 and 16384 gives 192 planned calls, one sample per cell. Both caps apply to every root. The higher value remains conditional on future endpoint support, admission, authorization and a complete prospective quote. Execution is disabled; the paid collector and dispatch deadline are null. The proposed additional 600 RUB ceiling is unquoted and unauthorized, with existing commitments retained.

Primary support remains 256 observed whitespace words, with 128/512 secondary. Eligibility retains exact model/provider, accepted visible reasoning, `finish_reason=stop`, gradeable final answer and the existing answer-marker censor. The common-cap engineering rule retains at least three error and three correct roots per model/cap, at least 36/48 completed gradeable attempts, and resolution of the complete fixed matrix without transport unknowns. A mixed root contributes to both class counts while remaining one resampling unit. No allocation extends until a gate passes, and no minimum verbosity or padding is requested to force prefix eligibility.

These formulations and caps differ from v2. A within-design cap comparison describes the new benchmark; it does not causally attribute historical v2 failures to task formulation versus censoring. Equal seeds across provider calls do not establish identical underlying trajectories or license treating a longer-cap answer as the continuation of a particular truncated response.

## Compact answers with auditable reference work

| Domain | Proposed construction tiers | Requested answer and independent check |
|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic | Conditional modular recurrences of 12/24/36 steps | Exact final integer; separately implemented recurrence replay and intermediate-state certificate |
| Logic | Exact assignment counts for 5/7/9 variables, targeting 7/11/15 clauses | Integer count; independent exhaustive truth-table enumeration |
| Algorithms | Weighted DAGs with 8/12/16 nodes, allowing negative weights | Shortest-path cost or impossibility; independent topological dynamic programming and bounded relaxation |
| Planning | 6/8/10 grids with 1/2/3 ordered keys | Shortest distance or impossibility; independent augmented-state search and shortest-distance certificate |

Compact final answers reduce required answer serialization; they do not guarantee short reasoning, completion or model mistakes. The construction schedule measures task properties rather than validated model difficulty. Logic must count assignments to every declared variable, including unused variables. Clauses and literal conventions must be explicit. Reference feasibility, zero/nonzero counts and solver work are reported as task properties, never as balanced model correctness.

Prompts define permanent keys, initially empty inventory, free pickup on entry, and checking a door before picking up a co-located key. Ordered key dependencies and a goal door require collection of all keys. Grid state-space bounds are 72/256/800 cells-times-key-masks; realized primary BFS counts range from 60 to 323. Arithmetic explicitly tests the old value and uses nonnegative modular remainders. CNFs are sampled without replacement from a finite signed three-variable-clause universe. Reference-only root selection allows at most 64 candidates; infeasible grids are rejected before any model outcome.

Current DAGs include a start-to-goal chain, accepted grids are reachable, and all preview CNFs have positive assignment counts. These are disclosed reference properties, not evidence of model success or balanced model errors. All 48 answers agree between the two implementations, and targeted tests plus deterministic rebuilding pass. A read-only third implementation also checked arithmetic histories, full assignment bitmaps, all-path DAG distances and complete grid distance maps for this preview. This corroborates the finite inputs; it is not human scientific review or proof for every future generated input.

## Root dependence, freezing and release

Presentation variants preserve the same latent problem and remain in one root. The [overlap audit](../artifacts/pilot-v3-design-2026-11-08/overlap-audit.json) checks historical normalized prompts, legacy CNF instances and structural flags, and unweighted projections of weighted DAGs. Arithmetic checks exact new programs; grid structural checks cover rotations among candidates, without a legacy key/grid semantic-equivalence proof. Shared templates are not independent families. The absence of detected overlap does not establish exhaustive novelty or root independence.

The public development seed is distinct from the existing private future-holdout commitment. The reserved seed is not consumed, and no final test is released. Root IDs bind generator version and seed; proposed cell IDs bind the actual corpus digest. The [source inventory](../artifacts/pilot-v3-design-2026-11-08/source-inventory.json) uses CRLF-to-LF normalization for portable offline identity; it is explicitly not an executable paid-source closure. Before future model data, actual raw sources, protocol, corpus, references, allocation, seeds and analysis rules require a new immutable binding, including a raw-protocol namespace for call identity. The original ledger and reserves remain cumulative. A paid condition needs a new valid deadline, authorized budget, current licenses, exact route and trace admission, and bound I/O compatibility. No automatic follow-on allocation is permitted after failure.

## Separate E0 readiness work

Subsequent user steering transferred the current development annotation to the separate [machine-only measurement](MACHINE_DEVELOPMENT_NOTE.md). The human handoff below remains prepared and deferred, with no human submissions or gold. This change does not enable v3 task generation or pass human E0.

The new [human development handoff](../design/E0_HUMAN_DEVELOPMENT_HANDOFF_2026_11_08.md) and [readiness record](../artifacts/e0-human-development-2026-11-08/readiness.json) prepare 24 exposed traces and 200 S2 role segments, with zero human submissions and no released role round. They preserve the [human-reference protocol](../design/HUMAN_REFERENCE_PROTOCOL.md), [annotation instructions](../design/ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL.md) and [preliminary E0 targets](../design/E0_PRELIMINARY.json). This package cannot meet the 40-root evaluation floor or supply unopened validation roots. Role coverage remains unknown. Constructed controls are not human gold, and agreement alone would not complete all six E0 checks.

Annotators require original reasoning with exact code-point offsets and CRLF preserved. The separate API answer is hidden; conclusions already inside reasoning remain, unlike predictor-prefix censoring. Two real humans independently annotate boundaries before receiving role packets; agreement precedes adjudication. Recipient metadata are masked, but public coordinator mappings and self-identifying text prevent guaranteed blinding; owner blinding is not claimed. Empty templates remain empty until real annotation occurs. No machine vote or programming audit substitutes for human measurement validity.

No final N or scientific utility margin is established here. The [existing planning scenarios](../design/PILOT_V2_STATISTICAL_PLAN.md) remain conditional illustrations, not empirical power. Even successful engineering coverage would not validate functional states, a transferable detector, causal stopping, E0 or G2–G4.
