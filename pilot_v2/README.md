# Development support pilot v2

This package is a separately versioned continuation after the closed observable-prefix pilot. Its first phase measures whether a fixed task design supplies completed correct and incorrect answers at prospective prefixes. It does not fit an error detector or validate reasoning states.

The prospective contract is `design/PILOT_V2_PROTOCOL_2026_11_08.json`; chronology and authorization are in `reports/PILOT_V2_SESSION.md`. A draft protocol is not dispatchable. Source, corpus, roster and protocol must be frozen and offline-tested before any new paid call.

## Design

- 24 development roots: four programmed domains, three construction tiers, two independently drawn base problems per cell.
- Two presentation variants per root; they remain one dependency group.
- Two previously admitted model/provider identities, each crossed with output caps 2048 and 8192 tokens.
- One sample per task/model/cap: 192 planned calls, with every unsent and unavailable cell retained.
- Primary support at 256 observed whitespace words; all 128/256/512-word results retained.
- Only completed, gradeable, exact-route visible-channel responses enter main support. Length-censored outputs remain incomplete even if an answer appears.
- A finite engineering support gate precedes any further study design. This floor is not statistical power or validation of an error monitor.

The output cap is absent from task construction. Complexity settings and output budget are crossed separately; task feasibility is never called answer correctness. No v1 test responses or predictions are used for tuning. Legacy prompts/data are used only for overlap audits. All v2 phase-A roots are development/exposed and excluded from a future held-out evaluation.

## Scientific limits

Observable text does not establish complete or faithful internal computation. E0 remains unpassed; original H0-H7, human review and manuscript approval are unchanged. Named RNG variants do not establish new task mechanisms, and held-out-from-fitting model identity does not establish a genuinely unseen family after development.

Future sample-size calculations must state root-level event and loss-distribution assumptions. Sensitivity simulations are planning evidence rather than validated empirical power. A future holdout seed commitment is recorded before calibration outcomes; no future held-out model responses are generated in this phase.
